Career Any% route

The perfect route to start and master your speedrunning journey across the United States of America.

Prelude​

This section will not explain frame per frame how to drive throughout the run as you will need practice to get used to the physics, the scripted parts and the RNG. Not every event will be shown here. It means there is no such info to know, just complete them as fast as possible.

Play on Easy difficulty to avoid getting Perfect Run rewards after finishing events. This saves 2 seconds per event.

After finishing every final event of Stage 1 - 2 - 4 - 5 - 6 and 9, instead of continuing directly to the next stage, quit to the Main menu and Continue the Run again. This skips various unlock animations and the Stage complete animation.

Stage 1​

Complete the MOB escape event (1 - 1), watch the 3 hours cutscene and reach the Nob Hill event.

Nob Hill (1 - 2)

Choose the Ford Shelby GT500. Restart the event as soon as possible after the The Race Begins... load screen, it will skip you the talk between Sam and Jack.

Interstate 580 (1 - 4)

At the end of the event, quit to the Main menu and go back into the Run.

Run position : 195

Stage 2​

Ellery Lake (2 - 5)

Do the reset glitch.
At the end of the event, quit to the Main menu and go back into the Run.

Run position : 172

Stage 3​

Panamint Valley (3 - 1)

At the end of the event, there is a huge jump. Your speedometer needle will start to go anticlockwise while mid-air, and you will lose a lot of speed whe, you will land. To prevent this, use nitrous as the needle is about to drop and hold it until you land.

Interstate 15 (3 - 4)

Restart the event as soon as possible to skip the cutscene.

Las vegas Blvd (3 - 5)

Go OOB at the gas station section, turn around and finish the event without crashing.

At the end of the stage, choose the Lamborghini Gallardo Balboni after Northshore Rd (3 - 6).

Run position : 139

Stage 4​

Hwy 169 (4 - 2)

Restart the event as soon as possible to skip the cutscene.

Red Mountain Pass (4 - 4)

Since the OOB can be a bit tricky to perform here, you can complete the event normally as the OOB requires practice to not get lost.

Loghill (4 - 5)

At the end of the event, quit to the Main menu and go back into the Run.

Run position : 109

Stage 5​

Interstate 70 (5 - 1)

After the crash cutscene, spam your Reset button to do the reset glitch. Then go OOB and finish the event. Pay attention to traffic as you're driving on oncoming lanes, if you crash you'll be sent to the beginning of the event.

Independence Pass (5 - 5)

Restart the event when you can only see a black screen from the fade-in.

At the end of the event, quit to the Main menu and go back into the Run.

Run position : 85

Stage 6​

Commerce City (6 - 1)

Restart the event to skip the cutscene. Spam your Reset button to do the reset glitch. Then go OOB and finish the event.

Independence Pass (6 - 5)

At the end of the event, quit to the Main menu and go back into the Run.

Run position : 67

Stage 7​

Kennedy Expressway (7 - 3)

Restart the event when Sam said **Jack... I just got word. to skip the cutscene.

Lower Wacker (7 - 4)

At the end of the event, when you're asked to choose a new car after yours has been pounded, perform the **Unswap glitch** to keep your Gallardo.

Run position : 50

Stage 8​

Nothing to say here as the stage is pretty straight forward but it can be cancer to complete, especially the last one (Industrial District (8 - 5)).

Choose the Porsche 918 RSR Concept.

Run position : 26

Stage 9​

Interstate 68 (9 - 2)

Go OOB after the bridge but be careful with the cops roaming around you, they can be really annoying. Note that like 5 - 1, you'll drive on oncoming lanes so be careful to the traffic.

Sawmill Drive (9 - 4)

Go off the track at the tree section, before triggering the cop cutscene and take the huge shortcut OOB, then finish the event by cutting the whole part at the end.

At the end of the event, quit to the Main menu and go back into the Run.

Run position : 11

Stage 10​

Newark Bay (10 - 4)

Restart the event to skip the cutscene. Spam your Reset button to do the reset glitch. Then go OOB and finish the event. (Game softlock chance)

Downtown NYC (10 - 6)

Drive through the orange barrier before entering the subway tunnels. This event is heavily scripted as Marcus will always rubberband if you try to pass him except before passing the "finish line".

Run position : 1st

Congratulations ! You just drove across the USA in less than 4 hours (maybe 3 ?).
It's now time to start over and master the game. You didn't do all that just to stop now, did you ? 😉
