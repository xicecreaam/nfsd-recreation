# General Information

All the information needed to get the basics of the game.  
<font color="#D94C4C">**Reading this document is mandatory for you.**</font> For anything described in this guide, it is assumed that the elements described here have been taken into account.

## Controller or keyboard ?

**Both input devices** (XInput & DualSense) are OK to use to play the game. However, playing **with a controller** will be better in any way since you can smooth steer. Keyboard can be also used but you will lose time using one compared to a controller.

## Driving techniques

Unlike its predecessors, there is no `Perfect start` during race starting cooldowns (except for drags). You might see your speedometer turning green at some point when throttling but it's only placebo.

Try to smooth steer as much as you can without making too many rapid analog stick movements unless you have to take very sharp corners.  
> :warning: The physics in this game are very different compared to Most Wanted and Carbon. Cars feel more heavier, so you might want to get used to them.

The NOS in ProStreet is different : it comes with charges and you can’t use NOS charges one after another. You have to use it wisely, once all charges used, no more NOS for you.

### Automatic or manual gearbox ?

Like Carbon, you want to set your transmission but this time to **Manual Clutch**. Manual gearbox allows precise gear control and the clutch grants you a better control and speed. It's a steep learning curve, but once shifting becomes second nature, you will be definitely faster on the track.

## Tuning slider glitch

Tuning sliders don't reset when parts are downgraded or uninstalled. For example, on a Tier 1 car, install any Stage 1/2 Suspension, go to the `Tuning` menu and change some sliders. Now, go equip the Stock Suspension and update the blueprint. Ta-da you have now a tuned stock suspension.

This glitch is used on the Speed Challenge 911 Turbo to make it sticked to the track when you jump.

*A lot of others glitches/bugs are present in this game but they are not speedrun related, only visuals or OOBs we can't profit.*

## Game exploits

### "Handbrake" drifting

By using the handbrake, you can drift in a straight line or keep you drift line in a direction while driving in the opposite direction (make a left turn while drifting to the right). What does that mean ? If you set the `Game Assists` to `Casual`, the latter kind of breaks the drift physics. It's hard to explain with a text but casual assists want your car **to stay on the track** by using a **"suck" strength**. However, it isn't that strong, you can see it when you take a corner a bit slowly and suddenly you want to leave the track.

To get maximum drift score, you need to drift in one direction as much as possible. This way, performing this trick allows you to score a lot of points. This trick is used to dominate one single raceday with only one drift completed.

### Clutch tires burn-out

ProStreet introduces a new mechanic before doing a drag race : the Tire Heating. But instead of doing it the way the game wants you to do, you can reach the "**Max Grip!!**" trigger (by filling the `tires burn-out` bar) much easier by **holding the clutch button and the throttle** (like in Most Wanted). This way, you can easily stay in the `Green zone`, where you have to hit to optimal RPM range.

### Push abuse trick

Still on the drag topic, instead of racing the drag normally, you can "push" your opponent at the starting grid to force him to do a **False Start**. Doing this will also force you to make a **False Start** but the game will make you win the round *(as if the game was giving you an advantage here)*.

This trick is only viable during a **1v1 challenge** : while facing *Karol Monroe* or *Ryo Watanabe*. A video example might also help you here.
